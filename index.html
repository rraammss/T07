IF SOMETHING DOESN'T LOOK RIGHT

Blank or black screen

The 3D agenda needs graphics acceleration. Open your browser's system settings, turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, then relaunch:
chrome://settings/system · edge://settings/system · brave://settings/system. Firefox: Settings → Performance → Use hardware acceleration.

Brave shows nothing

Click the lion icon and set Shields to Standard (or off) for this site, then reload.

Still on an old version

Do a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows, Cmd + Shift + R on a Mac.

Videos on the boards don't play

Open the agenda from this website, not from a downloaded copy on your computer: browsers block the board videos for files opened from a folder. A slow network can also delay the first frame for a second or two.

Check your graphics

Open chrome://gpu (or brave://gpu). WebGL should say Hardware accelerated. Work laptops sometimes lock this; try a personal device.

Getting around

Click the glowing seal to open the box, then a path, then a track. Inside a room: the green ◀ ▶ arrows (← → keys) walk board by board, the blue ⏮ ⏭ buttons (Shift + ← →) skip straight to the next room, and the numbers 1–5 at the bottom (or keys 1–5) jump to another block. The red exit button (or Esc) leaves the path. On a phone, turn it sideways.
